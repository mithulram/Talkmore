# Code of Conduct

Talkmore is built in public for people with different backgrounds, accents, languages, abilities, and levels of development experience. We want participation to be safe, useful, and respectful.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and specific when giving feedback.
- Discuss the work and its impact, not the worth of the person proposing it.
- Welcome questions and assume unfamiliarity before bad intent.
- Respect privacy. Do not repost recordings, dictation text, logs, or personal information without clear permission.
- Accept a maintainer decision when a discussion has reached a conclusion.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, repeated disruption, and publishing another person’s private information are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Reporting

For ordinary project conduct concerns, contact the repository owner privately through their GitHub profile instead of opening a public issue. Security vulnerabilities should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be reviewed in good faith, with the minimum disclosure needed to resolve the concern.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior harms the project or its community.
